# UniFind Design Direction

## Three approaches

### Theme Name: Campus Signal
Very Brief Intro: A warm editorial campus-service identity using deep ink, paper neutrals, and a precise signal orange to make the experience feel trustworthy, human, and operational. The interface treats lost-and-found as a civic utility rather than a marketplace.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Quiet Archive
Very Brief Intro: A calm museum-archive direction with sage, parchment, and muted blue, emphasizing memory, care, and the dignity of returned belongings. The UI would feel softly catalogued and reflective.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Night Relay
Very Brief Intro: A dark, high-contrast operations console with electric accents, designed around fast scanning and high-confidence status transitions. It makes Smart Matching feel like a live signal system.
Probability: 0.02

## Selected approach: Campus Signal

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial utility: the rigor of a university registrar or library system, softened by the warmth of a community noticeboard and the clarity of a modern product dashboard.

### Core Principles
1. **Trust through structure.** Every screen should make status, ownership, and next steps legible at a glance.
2. **Warm precision.** Use an ink-and-paper foundation with a single confident signal color instead of decorative gradients or generic blue UI.
3. **Service over spectacle.** Keep the interface focused on recovering belongings, not social engagement.
4. **Human evidence.** Small notes, match scores, comments, and timelines should feel like useful clues in a careful process.

### Color Philosophy
Deep ink (#14221F) anchors the product like a library card catalogue. Warm paper (#F7F4EC) makes the network feel approachable and physical. Signal orange (#E95A2B) is reserved for actions, match confidence, and moments that need attention. Moss (#617C62) communicates safe progress and successful reunions, while a faded denim (#4E6670) provides a quiet secondary voice.

### Layout Paradigm
Use an asymmetric editorial shell: a slim persistent navigation rail on authenticated screens, wide content columns with offset cards, and a hero that places the main promise left while a tactile “signal board” sits right. Avoid uniform centered stacks; favor intentional alignment, split compositions, and clear horizontal reading paths.

### Signature Elements
- A recurring **signal line** motif: thin rules, small nodes, and directional arrows that echo the Lost → Match → Connect → Reunite journey.
- **Paper slips** for item reports and claims, with subtle offset shadows and clipped corner accents.
- Compact **status stamps** such as OPEN, PENDING, and RESOLVED ✓ as typographic badges rather than glossy pills.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like moving a case forward. Hovering reveals the next useful action, buttons press with a slight physical scale, and match cards expose evidence instead of theatrics. Every primary action should produce an immediate, calm confirmation.

### Animation
Use 180–260ms ease-out transitions for buttons, navigation, cards, and drawers. Stagger the hero signal steps by 60ms. Let match confidence bars grow in from zero only on first entrance, while status stamps fade and lift by a few pixels. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid looping decorative animation.

### Typography System
Display: **Fraunces** 500/600 for expressive editorial headlines and numeric match percentages. Body: **DM Sans** 400/500/600 for UI clarity. Use uppercase micro-labels with generous tracking for status stamps, a 14–16px body scale, 30–48px headline scale, and oversized display numerals only for confidence or platform stats.

### Brand Essence
UniFind is the private UIU network for turning lost belongings into verified reunions through structured reporting and smart matching. Personality: **reassuring, observant, quietly optimistic**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and human. CTAs are specific and active. Microcopy explains what happens next without sounding corporate or overly cheerful.

Example lines:
- “A better way back to what’s yours.”
- “Report the details. We’ll surface the signal.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a compact, text-free symbol made from two offset rounded rectangles and a central notch: one slip represents a lost item, the other a found item, and the notch is the point where they reconnect. The wordmark uses a custom-feeling Fraunces treatment with a slightly lowered “F” crossbar.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Orange #E95A2B** — ownable, legible on warm paper, and reserved for actions that move a case forward.
